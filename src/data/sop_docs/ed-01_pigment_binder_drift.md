# SOP-ED01-006: Pigment/Binder Ratio Drift — E-Coat Bath (ED-01)

**Severity:** MEDIUM | **Tank:** ED-01 E-Coat Bath | **Est. fix:** 30-40 mins | **Rev:** 2.1

## 1. Description

The pigment-to-binder (P/B) ratio must be held at 0.18-0.25 for correct corrosion protection
and appearance. Low P/B (< 0.17) causes insufficient pigment in the film — poor UV resistance
and colour weakness. High P/B (> 0.27) causes over-pigmented, chalky film with poor gloss and
chip resistance. The ratio drifts as pigment and binder are consumed at different rates.

## 2. Sensor Thresholds

| Sensor | Normal | Warning | Alarm |
|--------|--------|---------|-------|
| pigment_binder_ratio | 0.18-0.25 | 0.16-0.18 or 0.25-0.28 | < 0.16 or > 0.28 |
| solids_pct | 18-22 % | | |

## 3. Corrective Action

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| P/B low (< 0.18) | Add pigment paste (Cathoguard B-Paste) 8 kg per 10,000L |
| P/B high (> 0.25) | Add binder solution (Cathoguard A-Binder) 12 kg per 10,000L |

## 5. Verification

1. P/B ratio 0.19-0.23 by lab analysis (2h turnaround).
2. Stone chip test panel — minimum 5 rating per VDA 621-427.

## 7. Related SOPs

- SOP-ED01-004 (Solids Depletion), lab call required for P/B confirmation.
