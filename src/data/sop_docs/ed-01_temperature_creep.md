# SOP-ED01-001: Temperature Creep — E-Coat Bath (ED-01)

**Severity:** HIGH | **Tank:** ED-01 E-Coat Bath | **Est. fix:** 30-45 mins | **Rev:** 3.5

## 1. Description

E-coat bath temperature above 32°C causes resin coagulation, reduced film build, surface defects
(orange peel, pinholes) and solids depletion through precipitation. Temperature creep is typically
gradual (0.5-1°C/hour) due to heat generated by the electrodeposition process itself. At 35°C,
film thickness drops 30% and solids begin precipitating as white resin agglomerates. All bodies
above 33°C threshold must be quarantined for film thickness check.

## 2. Sensor Thresholds

| Sensor | Normal | Warning | Alarm |
|--------|--------|---------|-------|
| temperature_c | 28-32°C | 32-33.5°C | > 33.5°C |
| solids_pct | 18-22 % | 17-18 % | < 17 % |
| voltage_v | 200-350 V | 350-380 V | > 380 V |
| conductivity_us_cm | 1200-1800 | 1800-2000 | > 2000 |

## 3. Immediate Actions

1. Switch chillers to maximum cooling immediately.
2. Reduce rectifier voltage by 10% to reduce heat input from electrodeposition.
3. Quarantine all bodies processed at > 33°C — film thickness measurement required.

## 4. Corrective Action

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Temp 32-33.5°C | Max chiller + reduce voltage 10% |
| Temp > 33.5°C | Halt production, max cooling, check chiller unit |
| Solids dropped < 17% | Add ED paint replenisher after temperature correction |

## 5. Verification

1. Temperature 29-31°C stable for 20 minutes.
2. Solids > 18.5% by non-volatile matter test.
3. Film thickness on first post-correction body: 18-25 microns.
4. Check pH — temperature rise often shifts pH down.
