# SOP-ED03-001: Membrane Fouling — UF Rinse 2 (ED-03)

**Severity:** LOW | **Tank:** ED-03 UF Rinse 2 | **Est. fix:** 60-120 mins | **Rev:** 2.0

## 1. Description

ED-03 is the second UF rinse stage providing the final paint recovery and rinse before the
DI water final rinse. Membrane fouling in ED-03 is detected by solids above 1.5% or
conductivity above 800 uS/cm. Fouling here has a less immediate impact than ED-02 but
degrades final rinse water quality, eventually affecting e-coat surface appearance.

## 2. Sensor Thresholds

| Sensor | Normal | Warning | Alarm |
|--------|--------|---------|-------|
| solids_pct | 0.2-1.5 % | 1.5-2.5 % | > 2.5 % |
| conductivity_us_cm | 200-800 | 800-1000 | > 1000 |

## 3. Corrective Action

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Solids 1.5-2.5% | Schedule CIP within 1 week |
| Solids > 2.5% | CIP immediately (SOP-ED-CIP-001) |

## 5. Verification

1. Solids < 1.0%, conductivity < 700 uS/cm.

## 7. Related SOPs

- SOP-ED-CIP-001, SOP-ED02-001.
