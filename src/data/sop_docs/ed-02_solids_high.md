# SOP-ED02-002: Solids High — UF Rinse 1 (ED-02)

**Severity:** LOW | **Tank:** ED-02 UF Rinse 1 | **Est. fix:** 20-30 mins | **Rev:** 1.6

## 1. Description

ED-02 solids above 5% indicate carry-over of e-coat paint from ED-01, either due to excess
drag-out or insufficient UF separation. High solids in ED-02 waste paint resin and can cause
streaking defects on subsequent bodies if the rinse is reapplied to the body surface.

## 2. Sensor Thresholds

| Sensor | Normal | Warning | Alarm |
|--------|--------|---------|-------|
| solids_pct | 1.0-5.0 % | 5.0-7.0 % | > 7.0 % |

## 3. Corrective Action

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Solids 5-7% | Increase UF permeate recirculation rate |
| Solids > 7% | Check SOP-ED02-001 (Membrane Fouling) |

## 5. Verification

1. Solids < 4.5%.

## 7. Related SOPs

- SOP-ED02-001, SOP-ED01-004.
