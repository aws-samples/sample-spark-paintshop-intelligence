# SOP-PT08-002: Concentration Low — Nano-Seal Tank (PT-08)

**Severity:** MEDIUM | **Tank:** PT-08 Nano-Seal | **Est. fix:** 20-30 mins | **Rev:** 1.9

## 1. Description

Nano-seal concentration dropping below 0.3% reduces the available active component for
conversion coating. This happens through normal consumption plus dilution from carry-over
water from PT-07. At 0.25%, coating coverage becomes patchy — visible as uneven iridescence
rather than uniform golden/green appearance on the body surface.

## 2. Sensor Thresholds

| Sensor | Normal | Warning | Alarm |
|--------|--------|---------|-------|
| concentration_pct | 0.3-1.2 % | 0.25-0.3 % | < 0.25 % |
| ph | 4.0-5.0 | 5.0-5.3 | > 5.3 |

## 3. Corrective Action

| Condition | Dose |
|-----------|------|
| Conc 0.25-0.30% | 1.5L Bonderite NT-1 per 10,000L |
| Conc < 0.25% | 2.5L Bonderite NT-1 per 10,000L, halt production |

## 5. Verification

1. Concentration 0.4-0.7%.
2. Visual check on first body — uniform iridescent coating.

## 7. Related SOPs

- SOP-PT08-001 (Passivation Failure), SOP-PT07-001.
